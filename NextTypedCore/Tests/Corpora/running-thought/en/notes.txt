Thoughts about the move, unsorted

The box of cables is not coming. I have carried it three times and never opened it, and at some point that is an answer. I kept the same box after the last move because you always need cables, and then in four years I used exactly one power supply out of it.

Flat: bright, third floor, no lift.
Landlord: reachable, answers late.
Kitchen: stays, which was the actual reason.

What surprised me is how much time goes into decisions that are not decisions. Whether the books go by colour or by subject genuinely does not matter, and I still spent half an evening on it. The decisions that cost something take ten minutes, because there is usually only one sensible option.

Still to sort out:

Transfer the internet
Mail redirection
A date for the handover
Deposit on the old flat

I underestimated the bedroom window. It faces east, which in summer means the day starts at half past five whether you agree or not. A blind solves it, but it only solves it once it is up, and until then you get up early and claim it was deliberate.

I met the neighbours on the second day, on the stairs, both carrying shopping, which is a good occasion because nobody has to talk for longer than they want to. They asked whether I was the one who had taken over the kitchen. Apparently that had been a topic in the building.

Verdict after a week: it was right. The rent is higher, the commute is shorter, and the box of cables is still in the hall.
